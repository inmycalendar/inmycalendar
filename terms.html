Governments move holidays, declare new ones at short notice, and observe them differently by region, by religion and by employer. Dates that depend on a lunar calendar or a moon sighting are especially likely to shift.

Do not use these dates as the basis for anything that matters - booking non-refundable travel, planning payroll, calculating notice periods, meeting a filing deadline, or any legal or employment decision. Check the official source for the country concerned. No responsibility is accepted for a date being wrong, missing or out of date.
