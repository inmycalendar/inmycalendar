Privacy

Short version: nothing leaves your browser unless you sign in

If you do not sign in

Nothing is collected. No account, no server, no analytics. Your tasks, day notes, day colours, countdowns and settings are saved in this browser's local storage, on this device, and nothing you type is transmitted anywhere.

Clearing your browser's site data, or using Clear data in the footer, deletes everything permanently. If you are not signed in there is no copy elsewhere to restore from, so use Backup first if you might want it later.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. Its only purpose is to sync your board across your devices.

Sign-in is handled by Supabase, using whichever provider you choose - Google, Microsoft, GitHub, or a one-time link sent to your email. We receive your email address and a user ID from that provider. We never see or store your password.

Once signed in, your tasks, day notes, day colours, countdowns and settings are stored in a database hosted in the EU (Frankfurt) so they can reach your other devices. That data is yours: it is readable only by your own account, enforced by the database itself, and it is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

You can sign out at any time, and you can ask for your account and its data to be deleted by emailing hello@inmycalendar.com.

Backups you make yourself

Backup and Export tasks write a file to your own device. What happens to it after that is up to you; it is not uploaded anywhere.

Public holiday data

Holiday dates are files served from this site. Choosing a country loads one small file; it does not tell anyone where you are, and no location data is collected.

Fonts

Two typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request, the same as on most sites using web fonts. Nothing about your tasks or notes is included.

Cookies and tracking

No tracking or advertising cookies are used. Local storage is not a cookie and is not sent with network requests. If you sign in, a session token is stored in your browser so you stay signed in; it does nothing else.

If this changes

Advertising is planned for a later version. It would change what is collected, and this page will be updated before it goes live. Advertising will also require a consent banner for visitors in the EU and UK.
