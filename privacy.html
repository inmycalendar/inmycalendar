Privacy

Short version: your data never leaves your browser

What is collected

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no server. Nothing you type is transmitted anywhere.

Where your data is stored

Your tasks, priorities, day notes, colour labels, tracked dates and settings are saved in this browser's local storage, on this device. They stay there until you clear them.

Clearing your browser's site data - or using Clear all data in the settings menu - deletes everything permanently. There is no copy elsewhere to restore from, so export a backup first if you might want it later.

Backups you make yourself

Export writes a file to your own device. What happens to it after that is up to you; it is not uploaded anywhere.

Fonts

Two typefaces load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request - the same as on most sites using web fonts. Nothing about your tasks or notes is included.

Cookies and tracking

None are used. Local storage is not a cookie and is not sent with network requests.

If this changes

Sign-in, cloud sync and advertising are planned for later versions. Each would change what is collected, and this page will be updated before any of them goes live. Advertising will also require a consent banner for visitors in the EU and UK.
