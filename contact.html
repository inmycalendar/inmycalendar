Reporting a bug

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • Board or calendar, and which scope - day, week or month
  • The date shown in the top bar when it happened
  • What you clicked, and what happened instead of what you expected
  • Your browser, and whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer

If your data looks wrong, use Backup in the footer first. That snapshot makes the problem far easier to reproduce.
